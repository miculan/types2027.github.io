## Venue

### The host

TYPES 2027 is hosted by the **University of Udine** (Università degli Studi di
Udine), founded in 1978 as part of the reconstruction of Friuli after the 1976
earthquake, and in particular by its Department of Mathematics, Computer Science
and Physics.

### The city

Udine is the historical capital of Friuli, in the north-east corner of Italy,
close to both Slovenia and Austria. It is a compact city of about 100,000
inhabitants: the centre is walkable end to end in twenty minutes, and the hills
of the Alpine foothills and the Adriatic coast are each less than an hour away.

The centre is built around **Piazza Libertà**, often described as the finest
Venetian square on the mainland, and the **castle** on the hill above it, which
looks out over the Friulian plain to the Julian Alps. Udine was the city of
**Giambattista Tiepolo**, whose frescoes can be seen in the cathedral, in the
Oratorio della Purità and in the Patriarchal Palace.

### Food

Friuli sits where Italy, the Alps and central Europe meet, and the food shows
it: San Daniele ham, Montasio cheese, frico, game and polenta, alongside the
white wines the region is known for. The centre of Udine keeps the habit of the
*tajut*, a small glass of wine taken standing at the bar in the late afternoon.

### Weather

June in Udine is warm: daytime temperatures are typically between 22 and 30 °C,
with the occasional thunderstorm rolling down from the mountains. Light clothing
and an umbrella are both sensible.

## Travel

### By air

The nearest airport is **Trieste Airport (TRS)**, at Ronchi dei Legionari, which
has its own railway station: trains reach Udine in about 35 minutes. APT bus
line G51 runs to Udine as well, taking 40 minutes when it goes non-stop on the
motorway and about an hour otherwise. Other options, roughly in order of
convenience:

| Airport | Distance | Connection to Udine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trieste (TRS) | 45 km | Train from the airport station, about 35 min; or APT bus G51, 40-60 min |
| Venice Marco Polo (VCE) | 130 km | Bus or taxi to Mestre, then train, about 2 h 30 |
| Venice Treviso (TSF) | 120 km | Bus to Treviso or Mestre, then train, about 2 h 30 |
| Ljubljana (LJU) | 150 km | Bus or car, about 2 h 30 |
| Klagenfurt (KLU) | 140 km | Car, about 2 h |
| Milan, Rome, Munich, Vienna | — | Long-distance trains via Venice, Verona or Villach |

### By train

Udine is on the line from Venice to Austria and is served by direct regional and
Frecciarossa/Intercity trains. Indicative direct journey times:

- Venice (Mestre or Santa Lucia): about 1 h 30
- Trieste: about 1 h 15
- Milan: about 4 h
- Vienna and Salzburg: direct daytime and night services via Villach
- Ljubljana: via Villach or Trieste

Tickets are sold by [Trenitalia](https://www.trenitalia.com/) and, on some
routes, by [Italo](https://www.italotreno.com/). The station, Udine Centrale, is
a fifteen-minute walk from Piazza Libertà.

### By road

Udine sits on the A23 motorway (Palmanova-Tarvisio), which joins the A4
(Turin-Trieste) at Palmanova, 25 km south. Coming from Austria, the A23 runs
down from the Tarvisio pass; from Slovenia, the border crossings at Gorizia and
Fernetti are within an hour.

### Getting around the city

Udine is small enough to cross on foot. City buses are run by
[TPL FVG](https://tplfvg.it/), and a regional day ticket covers buses and
regional trains across Friuli-Venezia Giulia, which is useful for excursions to
Cividale, Aquileia or the coast.

### Practical advice

Udine is a compact city and most hotels in or near the historic centre are
within twenty minutes' walk of the station and the main square. Early June is a
pleasant but busy time in Friuli, so booking a couple of months ahead is wise.

When choosing, note that:

- the **historic centre** (around Piazza Libertà, Via Mercatovecchio and Piazza
  Matteotti) puts the restaurants and the evening life on your doorstep;
- the area **around Udine Centrale station** is convenient for day trips and for
  early departures, and is a fifteen-minute walk from the centre;
- once the venue is confirmed, this page will say which neighbourhoods are most
  convenient for reaching it in the morning.

Besides hotels, there is a reasonable supply of bed and breakfasts and
short-term apartments in the centre, and the usual booking platforms cover the
city well. Student accommodation options, if available in June, will be listed
here.
