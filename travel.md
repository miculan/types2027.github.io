## By air

The nearest airport is **Trieste Airport (TRS)**, at Ronchi dei Legionari, which
has its own railway station: trains reach Udine in about 35-45 minutes. Other
options, roughly in order of convenience:

| Airport | Distance | Connection to Udine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trieste (TRS) | 45 km | Train from the airport station, 35-45 min |
| Venice Marco Polo (VCE) | 130 km | Bus or taxi to Mestre, then train, about 2 h 30 |
| Venice Treviso (TSF) | 120 km | Bus to Treviso or Mestre, then train, about 2 h 30 |
| Ljubljana (LJU) | 150 km | Bus or car, about 2 h 30 |
| Klagenfurt (KLU) | 140 km | Car, about 2 h |
| Milan, Rome, Munich, Vienna | — | Long-distance trains via Venice, Verona or Villach |

## By train

Udine is on the line from Venice to Austria and is served by direct regional and
Frecciarossa/Intercity trains. Indicative direct journey times:

- Venice (Mestre or Santa Lucia): about 1 h 30
- Trieste: about 1 h 15
- Milan: about 4 h
- Vienna and Salzburg: direct daytime and night services via Villach
- Ljubljana: via Villach or Trieste

Tickets are sold by [Trenitalia](https://www.trenitalia.com/) and, on some
routes, by [Italo](https://www.italotreno.com/). The station, Udine Centrale, is
a fifteen-minute walk from Piazza Libertà.

## By road

Udine sits on the A23 motorway (Palmanova-Tarvisio), which joins the A4
(Turin-Trieste) at Palmanova, 25 km south. Coming from Austria, the A23 runs
down from the Tarvisio pass; from Slovenia, the border crossings at Gorizia and
Fernetti are within an hour.

## Getting around the city

Udine is small enough to cross on foot. City buses are run by
[TPL FVG](https://tplfvg.it/), and a regional day ticket covers buses and
regional trains across Friuli-Venezia Giulia, which is useful for excursions to
Cividale, Aquileia or the coast.
