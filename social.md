The social programme follows the shape the series has kept for years: a
reception on the first evening, an excursion in the middle of the week, and the
conference dinner towards the end.

Udine is well placed for a half-day excursion. Cividale del Friuli, a UNESCO
site twenty minutes away by train, and the wine hills to the east are the
obvious candidates; see the [practical information]({{ '/practical/' | relative_url }})
page for what the surroundings offer.
