## Practical advice

Udine is a compact city and most hotels in or near the historic centre are
within twenty minutes' walk of the station and the main square. Early June is a
pleasant but busy time in Friuli, so booking a couple of months ahead is wise.

When choosing, note that:

- the **historic centre** (around Piazza Libertà, Via Mercatovecchio and Piazza
  Matteotti) puts the restaurants and the evening life on your doorstep;
- the area **around Udine Centrale station** is convenient for day trips and for
  early departures, and is a fifteen-minute walk from the centre;
- once the venue is confirmed, this page will say which neighbourhoods are most
  convenient for reaching it in the morning.

Besides hotels, there is a reasonable supply of bed and breakfasts and
short-term apartments in the centre, and the usual booking platforms cover the
city well. Student accommodation options, if available in June, will be listed
here.
