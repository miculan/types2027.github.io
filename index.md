# TYPES 2027

Welcome to the website of **TYPES 2027**, the 31st International Conference on
Types for Proofs and Programs.

The conference will be held in **Udine, Italy**, from **June 7 to June 11,
2027**.

## Important dates

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Abstract submission deadline | February 19, 2027 |
| Author notification | March 22, 2027 |
| Early registration deadline | April 12, 2027 |
| Camera-ready version deadline | April 30, 2027 |
| Late registration deadline | May 17, 2027 |
| TYPES 2027 Conference | June 7–11, 2027 |

## Venue

TYPES 2027 will take place in **Udine, Italy**.
